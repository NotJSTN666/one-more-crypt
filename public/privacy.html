Privacy Policy

One More Crypt · Last updated 6 November 2026

One More Crypt is a free browser game made by an independent developer. This page explains what the game stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. If you have any question, or want your data removed, email onemorecrypt@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without an account or an email address.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads or use ad trackers.
  • Names you choose and your run results are public on the leaderboards.
  • Most of your progress is saved in your own browser. If you choose a name, some of it is also kept on our server so it isn't lost.
  • You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (Souls, unlocks, settings, runs in progress and so on) in your browser's local storage, along with a random device id. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

What is sent to our server, and why

WhatWhy
A random device id (not linked to your real identity), the day, which game mode you played, how far you got, and the website that sent you here (for example "reddit" or "google")To count players and understand which parts of the game people enjoy or leave, so we can improve it
The name you choose, your run results (floor, kills, time, relics, hero and cosmetics) and the moves of each runTo show you on the leaderboards, check that scores are real, and replay runs
Your game progress and unfinished runs, if you have chosen a nameSo your progress isn't lost if your browser is cleared, and so you can continue on another device
What you are doing in the game right now (for example "Daily crypt")To show how many people are playing, and to send gifts to active players
Messages you send with the in-game Feedback button, with basic context (your name, screen, device type and a few game stats)To read and answer your feedback and fix bugs
Your email address, only if you sign in with email or turn on email remindersTo sign you in, and to send the reminders you asked for
A notification address from your browser, only if you turn on push remindersTo send the reminders you asked for

Like any website, our hosting services also see your IP address and browser type when you load the game. We don't use these to identify you.

What is public

Your chosen name, title, cosmetics and run results appear on the public leaderboards, and your Crypt Hall can be visited by other players. Your email address, device id and feedback are never shown to other players.

Services we use

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service, where the data above is stored.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the game's website.
  • Google Fonts: provides the game's fonts.
  • Resend: sends reminder emails, only if you asked for them.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google or Apple): delivers push reminders, only if you asked for them.
  • X (Twitter): only if you choose to sign in with X.
  • CrazyGames: if you play on CrazyGames, their own privacy policy also applies to their site and services.

Links to Discord, Ko-fi or X take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep leaderboard results and progress for as long as the game runs, so the boards and your progress stay intact. Player counts and statistics are kept in a form that isn't linked to your name. Feedback is kept until it has been dealt with. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without choosing a name: nothing is posted to the leaderboards under a name of yours.
  • Turn reminders off at any time in your profile, or with the link in any reminder email.
  • Clear your browser's data for the site to remove what is stored on your device.
  • Email onemorecrypt@gmail.com to see, correct or delete the data linked to your name or device. Tell us your in-game name so we can find it.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
