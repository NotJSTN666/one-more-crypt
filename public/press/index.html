About the game

One More Crypt is a free dark-fantasy roguelike that plays in the browser in seconds, on phone or PC.

Its heart is the Daily Crypt: one crypt a day, the same for everyone in the world, and you get one try. You descend floor by floor, stack cursed relics into broken builds, and see how deep you can get before Death comes for you. Then you compare with everyone else on the day's leaderboard. Think Wordle, but you're fighting your way down.

Around it the game has grown fast: an Endless mode, a daily Horde arena, the Night Shift survival mode, world-boss raids everyone fights together, and a story campaign set in a little town above the crypt.

Short version: a free daily roguelike in the browser. One crypt a day, the same for everyone, one try.

Features

  • The Daily Crypt: one shared crypt a day with its own leaderboard, a hero of the day, and a podium for the top three.
  • Endless: go as deep as you can, with your own hero, through deeper zones with their own looks and foes.
  • The Night Shift: endless survival in a graveyard. Relics fight for you, a boss rises every two minutes, and you dash through the crowd.
  • Horde: one arena a day, the same for everyone. Hold it against waves and chain kills.
  • The Bell Tolls: world bosses that everyone fights together at set times.
  • Story mode: the Hollow, a small town above the crypt, with quests, characters and two acts so far.
  • 8 heroes and 80+ relics, with fusions at the Soul Forge that turn two relics into one stronger one.
  • The Crypt Hall: a trophy room you can decorate and share, plus cloaks, hats and auras for your heroes.
  • Seasons: right now the Harvest Feast dresses the whole game for autumn until Thanksgiving, with Midwinter next.
