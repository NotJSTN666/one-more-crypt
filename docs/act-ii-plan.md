# Act II: The Plague Abbot (plan)

Status: teased in game ("Coming soon" card on the title screen). Not built yet.

## Where Act I left us

- The Bone Warden was never guarding a door. He was guarding a **lid**.
- The bell rang, the lid moved, and **Aldric was pulled down** with it.
- Mortimer: "The Plague Abbot's cloister lies under the Warden's door. If someone is
  ringing that bell, it's someone with a grudge and good shoulders."
- Finishing Act I unlocks the Bell-Ringer, the Great Bell aura and the Hall bell.

## Premise

Beneath the lid lies the **Sunken Cloister**, where the Plague Abbot's monks still keep
their vigil. Someone rings the bell every night now. Each toll, Gravesend's graves grow
restless and a green sickness creeps up through the village wells. The Abbot wants the
bell rung one last time, and wants the village to hear it.

## Shape (same as Act I)

- A new hub: **the Sunken Cloister**, reached through the Warden's door in the Hollow.
  Cloister walk, a herb garden gone wrong, a refectory, a scriptorium, the bell shaft.
- 4 main quests, 3 side quests.
- New zone look: plague-green mist, sputtering candles, wet stone (render only, like the
  deep looks, so replays stay safe).
- Finale: the Plague Abbot in the **Bell Tower Below**. Free Aldric.

## Characters

- **Brother Ambrose**: a monk who stopped praying when the others didn't stop dying.
  Kind, nervous, knows the cloister. Quest giver in the hub.
- **Mortimer**: still above ground, still sending you down.
- **Aldric**: heard but not seen until the finale.
- **Pip**, **Bess**, **Agnes**: the plague reaches the village; each gets a side thread.

## Main quests (draft)

1. **The Lid**: open the Warden's door properly, go down, meet Ambrose. Short, a tutorial
   for plague mist.
2. **Vespers**: the monks gather at every bell. Break up the evening service before the
   next toll. Choir of thralls, no boss, a big room fight.
3. **The Abbot's Physick**: find the Abbot's remedy book in the scriptorium. Choice:
   burn it, or bring it to Agnes (changes a line in the finale, and a reward).
4. **The Last Toll**: climb down the bell shaft to the Bell Tower Below. Boss: the Plague
   Abbot (phase 2 when the bell rings). Aldric freed.

## Side quests (draft)

- **Bitter Herbs**: replant the cloister garden for Ambrose (ties into Grave Shift crops
  if that's open).
- **Pip's Cough**: Pip falls ill. Fetch something from deep in the cloister.
- **The Silent Choir**: a monk who refuses to sing. Find his voice (a lost hymn page).

## New mechanic: plague mist

- Some story floors have mist tiles. Standing in mist for 2 turns stacks **Sickness**
  (−1 max HP for the rest of the floor, cap 3). Candles and the Bell-Ringer's toll clear
  mist around you.
- Story only. Endless and Daily do not change, so leaderboard runs and replays are safe.

## Rewards (story only, like Act I)

- A new hero for finishing the act (idea: **The Plague Doctor**, poison/cleanse kit).
- Looks: Monk's Habit, Plague Coat. Heads: Beaked Mask, Abbot's Cowl.
- Auras: Censer Smoke, Vespers Candles, the Unrung Bell.
- Titles: Brother of the Cloister, Physician, Abbot-Breaker, Hero of the Cloister.
- Hall: the Abbot's censer hanging beside the Great Bell.

## Build order

1. Hub map (Sunken Cloister) + Ambrose + door from the Hollow.
2. Plague look for story floors (render only).
3. Quests 1-2 + side quests, ship as "Act II, Part 1".
4. Mist mechanic, quests 3-4, finale, rewards. Ship as Act II complete.
5. Announcement news panel (like the Bell-Ringer one) for returning players.
