# Act II: The Plague Abbot (plan)

Status: **Part 1 built** (the Lid, the Sunken Cloister, Brother Ambrose, Vespers and three
side quests). Part 2 (the Physick, the Last Toll and the Abbot) comes next.

## Where Act I left us

- The Bone Warden was never guarding a door. He was guarding a **lid**.
- The bell rang, the lid moved, and **Aldric was pulled down** with it.
- Mortimer: "The Plague Abbot's cloister lies under the Warden's door. If someone is
  ringing that bell, it's someone with a grudge and good shoulders."
- Finishing Act I unlocks the Bell-Ringer, the Great Bell aura and the Hall bell.

## What we learned from Act I (October 2026)

- 20 players finished at least one quest, 9 finished Act I, and 8 of those 9 did every
  side quest. Story players are completionists: keep the side quests, and give them
  secrets to find.
- 45% of story starters finish the act, so the length is about right: 4 main quests,
  3 side quests, a hub with people in it.
- Act I asks the same thing of every dungeon ("clear the floors, beat the boss"). Act II
  should vary the objective from quest to quest.

## Premise

Beneath the lid lies the **Sunken Cloister**, where the Plague Abbot's monks still keep
their vigil. Someone rings the bell every night now. Each toll, Gravesend's graves grow
restless and a green sickness creeps up through the village wells. The Abbot wants the
bell rung one last time, and wants the village to hear it.

## Shape

- A new hub: **the Sunken Cloister**, down a stair from the Chapel Grounds once the lid is
  open. Brother Ambrose's cell, the Chapter House, the herb garden, the dormitory, the
  infirmary, and the bell shaft (sealed until Part 2).
- 4 main quests, 3 side quests, and one hidden quest (Part 2).
- New look for cloister dungeons: plague-green wet stone (render only).
- Finale: the Plague Abbot in the **Bell Tower Below**. Free Aldric.

## New mechanics (story only)

Endless, the Daily and Horde never change, so leaderboard runs and replays are safe.

- **Plague mist.** Patches of green mist on cloister floors. Foes inside the mist are
  hidden until you're right beside them. Standing in it hurts: 1 damage on every 3rd turn
  in a row you end there. Candles (the Votive Candle relic) burn the mist away around you
  each turn, and the Bell-Ringer's toll clears it in a wider ring, so Act I's reward hero
  shines here. Mist damage shows in the Last turns report.
- **Plague monks.** A new foe: slow to hit hard, and when one falls it leaves a puff of
  mist where it stood.
- **Hold-out floors.** The last floor of Vespers is a siege: the bell tolls and the
  monks pour in through the edges, wave after wave, before the stairs open.
- **Sleepers.** In the dormitory the monks are asleep and the stairs are open from the
  start. End a turn beside one and it wakes, and wakes the ones near it. Hitting one wakes
  it too. Sneak through, or fight.

## Main quests

1. **The Lid** (Mortimer, after Act I). Open the Warden's lid and go down. Light mist,
   the first plague monks. At the bottom: Brother Ambrose, and the cloister opens.
   *Built in Part 1.*
2. **Vespers** (Ambrose). The monks gather at every bell. Break up the evening service:
   the last floor is a hold-out, four waves of the choir. *Built in Part 1.*
3. **The Abbot's Physick** (Ambrose). Find the Abbot's remedy book in the scriptorium.
   Choice: burn it, or bring it to Agnes (changes a line in the finale, and a reward).
   *Part 2.*
4. **The Last Toll** (Ambrose). Down the bell shaft to the Bell Tower Below. On the way,
   ring the shaft's three bells in the order of the lost hymn (wrong order calls
   reinforcements). Boss: the Plague Abbot. Phase 1 rains telegraphed plague; at half
   health the bell rings, mist fills the room except a few safe spots, and he speeds up.
   Aldric breaks free and fights beside you at the end. A second choice: spare the old
   abbot or not, which changes the ending line and the title. *Part 2.*

## Side quests

- **Bitter Herbs** (Ambrose). The cloister garden has gone to rot and mist. Thick mist
  floors. *Built in Part 1.*
- **Pip's Cough** (Pip, in the village, once the lid is open). The sickness reaches the
  village. Fetch the Abbot's tincture from the infirmary. *Built in Part 1.*
- **The Silent Choir** (Ambrose, after Vespers). A monk who refuses to sing. Sneak
  through the sleeping dormitory to his cell and bring back the lost hymn page. Sleeper
  floors. The hymn page is the key to the bell order in Part 2. *Built in Part 1.*
- **The Fourth Bell** (hidden, Part 2). Found by exploring the cloister.
